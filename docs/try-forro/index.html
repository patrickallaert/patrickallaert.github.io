Try forró for free

Come try a free forró intro class and let yourself be bitten by the forró bug.

Forró is dance, rhythm, music and a party, all at once. This Brazilian folk dance from the north-east of the country is dynamic and joyful, sweet and sensual. Above all, it is fun.

Next free sessions

Our September and October sessions have ended. New opportunities to try forró are expected in January 2027, possibly as early as December 2026. Dates will be published here once confirmed.

No partner or previous dance experience is needed.
