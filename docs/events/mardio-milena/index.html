International guest workshops

Mardio & Milena

We’re very happy to welcome Milena Morais from Belo Horizonte and Mardio Costas from Salvador to Brussels for four special workshops.

Wednesday 9 September and Thursday 10 September 2026

  • Register for the workshops
  • View the programme

Workshop Programme

Four workshops across two evenings, with different levels and areas of focus.

Wednesday 9 September

Venue: Salle Dublin, Rue de Dublin 13, 1050 Ixelles

A palha do coqueiro

Time
19:00–20:15
Level
Intermediate
Prerequisite
Completed Level 2 twice, or a higher level

We’ll explore a different way of balancing the body, playing with imbalance, musicality, and movement.

O Molho

Time
20:30–21:45
Level
Open level
Prerequisite
Completed Level 2 once, or a higher level

We’ll work on one of Mardio and Milena’s fundamental techniques, O Molho, and apply it to movements.

Thursday 10 September

Venue: Dojo du Brochet, Rue du Brochet 55, 1050 Ixelles

Temperos do Nordeste

Time
19:00–20:15
Level
Open level
Prerequisite
Completed Level 1 or a higher level

We’ll explore the origins of forró and experience the characteristic movements of São João da Bahia through our bodies.

Adaptation: how to enjoy dancing with anyone

Time
20:30–21:45
Level
Advanced
Prerequisite
Completed Level 4, or danced intensively for four years and attended festivals

We’ll work on techniques and tools to connect with different people and body types, applying them to movements and exploring adaptation.
